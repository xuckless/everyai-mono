# Official sites: the domains a look-alike address most often imitates (package
# registries, git hosts, toolchains, vendor docs and downloads). An entry covers
# itself and every subdomain (pkg.go.dev is under go.dev). Keep entries to the
# registrable name (one label before the top-level domain): the look-alike check
# compares that label. Read by official_sites.zig (backend fetches) and copied
# into frontend/lib/core/security/official_sites.dart (the browser tool); a test
# fails when the two differ. `neighbours` are real sites that read as a typo of
# an official one; they are fine.
